# Date Turn

Run `pnpm dev` and open `/date-turn/`. The **설정** button or D key opens controls for the three numbers (1–6 digits per face, preserving leading zeros), size, pointer sensitivity, playback speed, motion, four colors, and outline width. Drag horizontally and vertically for free rotation; click or Space toggles automatic rotation. **현재 렌더 PNG 저장** exports the current canvas, including the chosen numbers, colors, size, and viewing angle.

**3D 디버그** opens a paused, enlarged inspection view; `/date-turn/?inspect` opens it directly. Drag to rotate, or use **1면 정면**, **2면 정면**, **3면 정면**, and **사선**. **음영 표면** shades the actual ray-marched surface; **표면 방향** encodes its object-space normal in RGB (X/Y/Z); **경계만** hides the solid and shows the three input planes. **표면 구성** isolates the rounded body or numeral supports using the same fields that construct the full volume. Solid colored lines show the raw glyph field's zero contour, including counters; dashed lines show the filled housing field offset by **숫자 둘레 여백**. They are drawn on the three face planes, not inferred from a screenshot. Toggle each layer, coordinate grids, and faded occluded contours independently. **설정 → 3D 디버그 → 디버그 확대** adjusts inspection magnification. Closing the inspector restores the previous playback time, drag rotation, and playing state.

The inspector exposes the current representation: glyphs are sampled into a 768×768 distance texture, and the body combines those inputs with an analytic rounded housing. These contours remain sampled distance-field contours, not original font Bézier paths. The coordinate grid is a diagnostic overlay, not a triangle mesh; the dashed housing inputs are not the final solid's silhouette. The inspector does not convert or replace the artwork's geometry.

Automatic motion rolls each face through 120° across a 0.56-second transition, centered on each half-second. A quintic easing curve gives the roll zero velocity and acceleration at both ends. Continuous ±32° yaw, ±13° screen tilt, and vertical movement share a three-second phase. The incoming numeral's ink starts with a distance-field inset and fills during the late settling phase; the outgoing ink stays filled. **전환 시간**, **좌우 회전 각도**, and **기울기 각도** tune the angular components. Playback follows elapsed time rather than a capped per-frame time step. Dragging uses 180° per viewport at sensitivity 1 and an exponential response; **드래그 부드러움** is the response time in seconds. The response keeps rendering while settling, including when automatic playback is paused.

The WebGL renderer generates numeral distance fields from the selected font and constructs a three-sided solid from those fields. Arial Black and Arial use system fonts with bundled Pretendard ExtraBold as fallback; Pretendard can also be selected directly. Numeral caps stay flat; their supports connect to a rounded white body. Each frame traces the actual rotated volume, resolves occlusion, draws the silhouette, and applies antialiasing. No reference frames, traced frame paths, or source video are used at runtime. The original remains a visual reference; the reconstructed font and body are not a recovered original model.

Closed holes in numerals such as `0` and `9` must not cut slots into the shared white body. When text or font changes, the CPU generates a second mask that fills only enclosed holes, keeping gaps connected to the exterior open. Glyph and housing distances occupy RG and BA of the same texture, so the GPU still samples three face textures per marching step. The housing fields and cap supports use local polynomial smooth unions to fill narrow joins; the result is clipped back to the three face planes. The original glyph field continues to determine the colored numeral and its holes.
