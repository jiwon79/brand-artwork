# Date Turn

Run `pnpm dev` and open `/date-turn/`. The **설정** button or D key opens controls for the three numbers (1–6 digits per face, preserving leading zeros), size, pointer sensitivity, playback speed, motion, four colors, and outline width. Drag horizontally and vertically for free rotation; click or Space toggles automatic rotation. **현재 렌더 PNG 저장** exports the current canvas, including the chosen numbers, colors, size, and viewing angle.

**3D 디버그** opens a paused, enlarged inspection view; `/date-turn/?inspect` opens it directly. Drag to rotate, or use the three frontal presets and **사선**. **음영 표면** shades the actual surface, **표면 방향** encodes object-space normals in RGB (X/Y/Z), and **경계만** shows the contours. **표면 구성** isolates the triangular prism or the extruded numbers. Solid colored contours lie on the numbers' outer caps; dashed rectangles lie on the prism's three base faces. Toggle contours, coordinate grids, and occluded lines independently. Closing inspection restores the previous time, rotation, and playing state. The coordinate grid is not a triangle mesh, and the glyph contours come from a sampled 768×768 distance field, not original font Bézier paths.

Automatic motion rolls each face through 120° across a 0.56-second transition, centered on each half-second. A quintic easing curve gives the roll zero velocity and acceleration at both ends. Continuous ±32° yaw, ±13° screen tilt, and vertical movement share a three-second phase. The incoming numeral's ink starts with a distance-field inset and fills during the late settling phase; the outgoing ink stays filled. **전환 시간**, **좌우 회전 각도**, and **기울기 각도** tune the angular components. Playback follows elapsed time rather than a capped per-frame time step. Dragging uses 180° per viewport at sensitivity 1 and an exponential response; **드래그 부드러움** is the response time in seconds. The response keeps rendering while settling, including when automatic playback is paused.

The model is one finite regular triangular prism and three straight numeral extrusions. The prism runs along X. Its side normals are `(0, 0, 1)`, `(0, √3/2, -1/2)`, and `(0, -√3/2, -1/2)`. Each glyph keeps its planar X/tangent coordinates and occupies the interval from the base face to **숫자 돌출 깊이** along that face's outward unit normal. A hard union joins these solids. There is no rounded housing, smooth blending, or counter filling. Each label’s measured glyph height is fitted exactly to the face height `2√3 × prismRadius`, and follows radius changes live. Its measured width independently fits the full prism length. Both dimensions follow prism changes live, stretching or compressing the font proportions to fill the face. Only the outer numeral caps receive the number color; walls and prism use the side color.

**설정 → 삼각기둥 · 숫자 돌출** controls the center-to-face distance (default 0.32), prism length (2.7), and extrusion depth (0.30). Text or font changes regenerate the raw glyph distance textures. Arial Black and Arial use system fonts with bundled Pretendard ExtraBold as fallback; Pretendard can be selected directly. Each frame ray-marches the rotated prism/extrusion union, resolves occlusion, draws the silhouette, and applies antialiasing. The font is an approximation of the supplied reference; no reference frames, traced paths, or source video are used at runtime.

The incoming numeral’s animated stroke inset is shared by the solid extrusion, cap color, and inspector contours. Its walls move with its cap boundary as the strokes fill during settling; there is no fixed wide extrusion behind a thinner painted numeral.
